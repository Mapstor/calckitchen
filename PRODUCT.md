# PRODUCT.md — calckitchen.com

## What it is
CalcKitchen: 20 free kitchen calculators (appliance converters, cooking times, baking, recipe tools) for US home cooks.

## Who uses it
A US home cook with a timing, temperature or quantity question, often mid-cooking and on a phone: "how long for a 16-lb turkey", "air fryer time for this oven recipe", "cups to grams for flour".

## The job
Give the answer first, correct and sourced, with the safe temperature where food safety is involved. Then the table, the worked examples and the reasons, for people who want to check.

## Goals
1. Google traffic from US English searches.
2. Being cited by AI assistants.
3. Raptive approval and display-ad income; the design keeps room for sidebar, in-content and sticky footer ads.

## Brand
A precise kitchen instrument: calm, exact, a little motion. Not a recipe blog, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Voice
Plain and direct, numbers first, no hype. US units first (°F, lb, cups), metric second. Every figure names its source (USDA FSIS first for food safety). No unverifiable claims about users or traffic.

## Trust
- Sources with working links on every page; registry in research/data-sources.csv.
- Author byline planned: Marko Visic, MPharm, BSc Physics (owner's sitewide standard; About page and author details still to build).

## Anti-references
Emoji icons, recipe-blog clutter, generic rounded card grids, cream + serif + terracotta, near-black + neon.
