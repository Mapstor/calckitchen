## Calculator Cards

Each card in the grid should show:
- **Icon** (emoji or simple SVG icon)
- **Calculator name** (short — "Air Fryer Converter")
- **One-line description** ("Convert oven recipes to air fryer")
- **Link** to the full page

Cards should be clickable, with hover effect. On mobile, 2 columns. On desktop, 4-5 columns.
