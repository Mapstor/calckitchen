# Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you've spotted a bug, have feedback on one of our calculators, or want to suggest a new tool — we're all ears.

**Email:** [hello@calckitchen.com](mailto:hello@calckitchen.com)

Here are a few things we especially appreciate hearing about:

- **Bug reports** — If a calculator is giving you unexpected results, let us know what inputs you used and what you expected. It helps us fix things fast.
- **New calculator ideas** — Is there a kitchen calculation you're always doing by hand? Tell us about it. Our best tools have come from user suggestions.
- **General feedback** — Love something? Hate something? We want to know.

We read every email and do our best to respond within a few business days. Thanks for helping us make CalcKitchen better for everyone.
