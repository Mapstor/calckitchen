# About CalcKitchen.com

## Kitchen Math, Solved.

There's a moment every home cook knows too well. You're standing in the kitchen, recipe in hand, staring at a 14-pound turkey and wondering — how long does this actually need to cook? Or you've just bought an air fryer and every recipe you find is for a conventional oven. Or you're halving a cake recipe and trying to figure out what "half of ¾ cup" actually looks like.

We've been there. And we built CalcKitchen to make sure nobody has to guess.

## Our Story

CalcKitchen was born out of a simple frustration: the math behind cooking shouldn't be harder than the cooking itself. Cook a roast too long and it's dry. Undershoot the internal temperature and you've got a food safety problem. Get the hydration wrong on your pizza dough and you'll end up with a cracker instead of a crust.

We believe cooking should be accessible, enjoyable, and stress-free — and that starts with getting the fundamentals right. No one should have to dig through five blog posts, scroll past someone's life story, and still not find a clear answer to "how long do I cook a 6-pound pork shoulder in a slow cooker?"

That's why we built a suite of free, fast, and reliable kitchen calculators — designed to give you the answer you need in seconds, so you can get back to making great food for the people you love.

## Built on Authoritative Data

We take accuracy seriously — because in the kitchen, a few degrees or a few minutes can be the difference between perfectly cooked and overcooked, between safe to eat and not.

Every calculator on CalcKitchen is built on data from trusted, authoritative sources:

- **USDA (United States Department of Agriculture)** — Our meat cooking temperatures, safe handling guidelines, and food composition data come directly from USDA recommendations. When we tell you a chicken needs to reach 165°F internally, that's the science-backed standard.

- **FoodSafety.gov** — For thawing guidelines, storage times, and safe cooking practices, we reference the federal interagency food safety guidelines.

- **Professional cooking standards** — Our conversion formulas, appliance adjustment factors, baker's percentages, and serving size guidelines are drawn from professional culinary industry standards, cross-referenced with real-world testing.

We update our data regularly. When we recommend you use a food thermometer (and we will — often), it's because no calculator can replace one. We give you the best possible estimate; the thermometer gives you the confirmation.

## Our Design Philosophy

**Fast answers, no friction.** Every CalcKitchen calculator is designed to get you from question to answer in seconds. No pop-ups. No "sign up to see your results." No walls of text before the tool. You come to the page, you use the calculator, you get your answer. Simple.

**Mobile-first.** We know where you are when you need a cooking calculator — in the kitchen, phone in one hand, spatula in the other. Every tool works beautifully on a phone screen, with large tap targets, clear inputs, and results you can read at a glance.

**Free forever, no registration required.** CalcKitchen is and will always be completely free to use. We don't require an account, we don't lock features behind a paywall, and we don't ask for your email to show you results. Every calculator, every conversion, every feature — free and open to everyone, always.

**Beautiful and functional.** We believe tools can be both useful and pleasant to use. Our calculators feature clean, modern design with visual elements — like SVG cut-line diagrams for cake servings and color-coded doneness indicators for meat — that make the information not just accessible but intuitive.
